### Task 2: Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

**Project Overview**

Developed an interactive Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance and identify useful business insights.

**Tools & Technologies**
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
- Total Sales
- Unique Customers
- Products Sold
- Sales Records
- Total Quantity
- Total Profit

**Dashboard Features**
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Sales analysis by category
- Top 10 sub-categories by sales
- Regional sales comparison
- Interactive filters and slicers
- KPI cards for tracking business performance

**Project Objective**

The objective of this project is to transform sales data into meaningful visual insights that help understand revenue trends, customer activity, product performance, and regional sales distribution.
